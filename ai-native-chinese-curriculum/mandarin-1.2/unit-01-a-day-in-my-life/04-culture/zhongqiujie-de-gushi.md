# 中秋节的故事 (The Mid-Autumn Festival Story)

## 定位说明
这是 Unit 1「Culture」模块的其中一个内容——因为本学年开学时间与中秋节临近，
所以今年 Unit 1 的文化内容选择了中秋节的故事（对应 Syllabus 中泛称的 "The Rabbit Story"）。
不是每年 Unit 1 都固定教这个故事，规则见 [01-overview.md](../01-overview.md#cultural_focus)。
本 Unit 的 Culture 模块下还有另一个内容：[书法 (Calligraphy)](./shufa-calligraphy.md)。

## Overview
A simplified "Cold Character Reading" retelling of the Mid-Autumn Festival legend, using
vocabulary students already know (see Text / Media below). An illustrated picture-book version
is also used in class as supplementary material.

Warm-up cultural questions:
- Do you know what day the Mid-Autumn Festival falls on?
- Who do you think appears in the story of the Mid-Autumn Festival?

Learning goal: I can talk about the story of the Mid-Autumn Festival

After reading, students write their own summary of the story using connector words (see
Activities below).

## Text / Media（正文或媒体内容：故事、新闻、音频、歌曲、视频均可）
Cold Character Reading version — a simplified retelling using vocabulary students already know,
Chinese with an English gloss on each line so family readers can follow along:
- 有十个太阳。— There were ten suns in the sky.
- 有一个男生，他是男生，他叫后羿。后羿是人。后羿很 lì hài。— There was a boy named Hou Yi. Hou Yi was human. Hou Yi was very mighty.
- 后羿杀太阳。— Hou Yi shot down the suns.
- 有一个太阳。没有十个太阳。— Now there was only one sun. There weren't ten suns anymore.
- 王母娘娘是神仙。王母娘娘不是人。— The Queen Mother of the West was an immortal, not a human.
- 王母娘娘谢谢后羿。王母娘娘给后羿仙丹。— The Queen Mother thanked Hou Yi and gave him an elixir of immortality.
- 可是，后羿有嫦娥。后羿不想要吃仙丹。后羿想要和嫦娥一起。— But Hou Yi had Chang'e. He didn't want to eat the elixir — he wanted to stay together with Chang'e.
- 后羿给嫦娥仙丹。后羿不想要吃仙丹。后羿爱嫦娥。后羿想和嫦娥一起。— Hou Yi gave the elixir to Chang'e instead. He loved her and wanted to stay with her.
- 可是，有一个坏人，坏人是人。坏人想要吃仙丹。— But there was a bad man, also human, who wanted the elixir for himself.
- 坏人没有仙丹。可是，嫦娥有仙丹。— The bad man didn't have the elixir. Chang'e did.
- 嫦娥不想要吃仙丹。嫦娥爱后羿。嫦娥想要和后羿一起。— Chang'e didn't want to eat it either — she loved Hou Yi and wanted to stay with him.
- 可是，坏人想要吃仙丹。坏人想要杀嫦娥。嫦娥很害怕。嫦娥吃了仙丹。— But the bad man wanted the elixir and threatened to kill Chang'e. Frightened, she ate it herself.
- 嫦娥是神仙。嫦娥不是人。后羿不是神仙，后羿是人。— Chang'e became an immortal. She was no longer human. Hou Yi remained human.
- 嫦娥去月亮。后羿不去月亮。— Chang'e went to the moon. Hou Yi could not go with her.
- 嫦娥哭了，嫦娥不想要去月亮，嫦娥想要和后羿一起。— Chang'e cried — she didn't want to go to the moon, she wanted to stay with Hou Yi.
- 嫦娥哭了。后羿也哭了。— Chang'e cried. Hou Yi cried too.

> Note: "lì hài" (厉害, "mighty/formidable") is intentionally left in Pinyin-only in the source
> reading — the character hasn't been taught yet, so the reading scaffolds it phonetically.

## Activities
- Listening: role play
- Speaking: movie talk
- Listening: "Pencil Grab" (拿铅笔) listening/judgment game (true/false; +1 point for a correct answer, minus 1 for incorrect)
- Writing: summarize the story on grid paper using connector words (可是/然后/所以)
- Homework: review new vocabulary (Quizlet link)
