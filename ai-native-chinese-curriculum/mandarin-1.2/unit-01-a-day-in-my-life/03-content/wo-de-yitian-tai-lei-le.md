# 我的一天太累了 (My Day Is So Tiring)

Story-specific vocabulary and full text confirmed 2026-08-04 (see Vocabulary / Text below). Its
place in the Cycle 1 / Cycle 2 teaching sequence still isn't confirmed — see 08-curriculum-intelligence.md
if a later upload clarifies it.

## Overview
A first-person letter written by a student ("李朋"/"王朋" — the source material uses both names
inconsistently, kept as-is) to his teacher 陈老师, describing a tiring, overwhelming month of
school: staying up late studying, sleeping poorly, dozing off in class, and accidentally
answering an English question in Chinese out of embarrassment/habit.

Related general Unit 1 daily-routine vocabulary (not story-specific) lives in
[the vocabulary resource](../05-resources/vocabulary-my-day.md).

## Text / Media（正文或媒体内容：故事、新闻、音频、歌曲、视频均可）
Chinese with an English gloss on each line so family readers can follow along:
- 陈老师，您好！好久没给您写信了，最近好吗？— Teacher Chen, hello! I haven't written to you in a long time — how have you been recently?
- 这个月我很忙，每天都学习到很晚，觉也睡得不好。早上起床以后，常常没时间吃饭就到教室去上课了。— This month I've been so busy, studying late every day, and not sleeping well either. After getting up in the morning, I often go straight to class with no time to eat.
- 今天我太累了，上电脑课的时候一边听着老师说话，一边就睡着了！— Today I was so tired that during computer class, I fell asleep while listening to the teacher talk!
- 老师叫了我的名字，用英文问问题的时候，我竟然用中文说"我懂了"，真是不好意思！— When the teacher called my name and asked a question in English, I unexpectedly answered "I understand" in Chinese — so embarrassing!
- 快到十二月了，真希望我可以早点儿回中国！— December is almost here — I really hope I can go back to China soon!
- 祝好！您的学生：王朋 — Best wishes! Your student, Wang Peng
