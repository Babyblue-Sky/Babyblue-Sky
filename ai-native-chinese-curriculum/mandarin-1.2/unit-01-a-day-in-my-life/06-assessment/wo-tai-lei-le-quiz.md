# 我太累了 Quiz

考试内容包括三部分：
- **词** — Interpret words/phrases from the story: write down their meaning and Pinyin (optional)
- **听句子 👂** — Write down the sentence Ms. Liao says
- **写句子 ✏️** — Compose sentences using the given words, phrases, or sentence structures

A matching review sheet ("我太累了 quiz review") with the same three sections is given to
students ahead of the quiz.

## 考查技能点 (Skills Assessed)
- Character recognition（汉字识别）
- Listening dictation（听写）
- Sentence composition（造句）
