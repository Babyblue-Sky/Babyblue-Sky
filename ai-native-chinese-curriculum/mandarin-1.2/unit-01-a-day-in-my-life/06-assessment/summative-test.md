# 熊猫的故事 Summative Test

考试内容包括三部分：
- **词** — Interpret words/phrases from the story: write down their meaning and Pinyin (optional)
- **句子** — Write a description based on given pictures from the story
- **说一说** — Ms. Liao asks 3-5 questions about your daily routine and/or the panda story, e.g.:
  - 你每天几点起床？
  - 你每天几点睡觉？
  - 你常常运动吗？
  - 熊猫在哪儿出生？
  - 熊猫想要吃什么？

A matching review sheet ("熊猫的故事 复习") with the same three sections is given to students
ahead of the test.

## 考查技能点 (Skills Assessed)
- Character recognition（汉字识别）
- Sentence writing（句子书写）
- Sentence reading（句子朗读）
- Speaking（说一说 short spoken responses）
