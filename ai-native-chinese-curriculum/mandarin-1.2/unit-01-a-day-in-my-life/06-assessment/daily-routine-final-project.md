# 健康还是不健康？(Healthy or Unhealthy?) — Daily Routine Final Project

## Driving Question
健康还是不健康？(Healthy or unhealthy?) — What does a healthy vs. an unhealthy daily routine
look like, and which one do you want to live?

## Instructions
We've learned how to describe our routines and what healthy vs. unhealthy lifestyles look like.
For the final project, create an E-book in Chinese characters covering:
- A short story about a character's healthy lifestyle (健康的生活方式)
- A short story about a character's unhealthy lifestyle (不健康的生活方式)
- What lifestyle you'd want to live, and why (我喜欢…因为…，at least three reasons)

Process:
1. Plan the project (bullet-point your ideas for each of the three parts above).
2. Draft by hand in paragraphs — Part 1 (healthy routine), Part 2 (unhealthy routine), Part 3 (which you'd choose and why).
3. Type your final draft in a Word document.
4. Build the E-book at bookcreator.com — sign in with your school Google account, join the class's Online Mandarin Library, then design your book (comic-style layout recommended for more design options).
5. Make sure the finished book has: a title, your name as author, all sentences from your final draft, and a picture on every page (or every other page).
6. Record yourself reading every page aloud.
7. Publish.

Due dates: final (handwritten) draft due Dec. 8 in class; finished E-book due and presented in class Dec. 18.
