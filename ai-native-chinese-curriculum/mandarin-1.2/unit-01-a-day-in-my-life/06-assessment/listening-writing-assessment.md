# 1.2 Listening & Writing Assessment 听一听和写一写考试

Three parts:

1. **Listening cloze** — listen to a short story and fill in the blanks using words from a
   given word bank (daily-routine vocabulary: time-of-day words, meals, 起床/睡觉/洗澡/回家/做作业).
2. **Sentence composition** — choose three words from a second word bank (上学/放学、上班/下班、
   开灯/关灯、前面/后面、去学校、回家、…的时候) and write one sentence with each.
3. **Paragraph writing: 我的一天** — describe your daily school-day schedule. Checklist:
   - Title the piece 我的一天
   - Answer: 你几点起床？你几点吃早饭？你几点去学校？你几点吃午饭？你几点回家？你几点吃晚饭？你几点做作业？你几点睡觉？
   - Optional (extension): 你吃早饭吗？你午饭喜欢吃什么？你晚饭喜欢吃什么？你在家喜欢做什么？你星期六和星期日喜欢做什么？
   - At least 10 sentences, all in Chinese characters
   - At least three connector words (using the classroom poster as reference)
   - Optional: also describe your weekend schedule

A reading-comprehension companion piece (about a student named Lily/丽丽, ~130 characters,
describing her daily routine) is provided with tiered comprehension questions — "ME" level
questions answerable in English, "EE" level questions requiring a Chinese-language response.

## 考查技能点 (Skills Assessed)
- Listening comprehension / dictation cloze（听力填空）
- Sentence composition（造句）
- Paragraph writing（段落写作）
- Reading comprehension, tiered by proficiency level（分层阅读理解）
