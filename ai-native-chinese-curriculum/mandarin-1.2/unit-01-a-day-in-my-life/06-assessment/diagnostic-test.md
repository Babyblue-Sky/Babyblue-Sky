# Mandarin 1.2 Diagnostic Test

评分对应的三条 Course Standard（每条按 EE/ME/AE/BE 四级评定）：
- **Interpretive Communication** — Learners understand, interpret, and analyze what is heard, read, received or viewed on a variety of topics, using a range of diverse texts, including authentic resources.
- **Interpersonal Communication** — Learners interact and negotiate meaning in spontaneous, spoken, visual, or written communication to exchange information and express feelings, preferences, and opinions.
- **Presentational Communication** — Learners present information and ideas on a variety of topics adapted to various audiences of listeners, readers, or viewers to describe, inform, narrate, explain, or persuade.

## Listening
Please listen to Ms. Liao carefully and mark the Pinyin for each character.

1. 昨天　2. 姐姐　3. 中国　4. 晚上　5. 三点一刻
6. 这个　7. 你好　8. 生日　9. 七年级　10. 星期六

Listen to Ms. Liao and write down what you heard in Chinese characters. (5 items)

## Reading
Read the paragraph about 小月 and answer the comprehension questions.
(You can answer in English but you will get extra credit if you answer in Chinese.)

> 你好！我叫小月，我今年十二岁，我上七年级。我家有五口人：爸爸，妈妈，两个姐姐和我。
> 我的爸爸是老师，我的妈妈是护士。我的姐姐和我是学生。我们是中国人。我和我的家人住在纽约。
> 我的生日是三月二十七号。

Comprehension questions:
1. How old is 小月? Which grade does she go to?
2. How many people does 小月's family have? Who are they?
3. Do 小月's mom and dad work? What do they do?
4. Where do 小月's family live?
5. When is 小月's birthday?

## Writing: My Family and I
Please write a short paragraph in Chinese characters introducing yourself and your family.
Feel free to use the paragraph about 小月 as a reference.

Suggested vocabulary: 岁，有，个/口，是，哥哥，姐姐，妹妹，弟弟，老师，律师，住在，纽约，
中国人，美国人，六年级，七年级，八年级

## Speaking
Ms. Liao will call on each student and have a short conversation in Chinese.
(Students should know Super Eight words and use them to answer questions.)
