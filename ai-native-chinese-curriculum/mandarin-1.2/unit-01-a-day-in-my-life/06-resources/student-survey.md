# 1.2 中文课调查问卷 (Chinese Class Student Survey)

> Welcome to Chinese class. I am 张老师 (zhāng lǎo shī), your Chinese teacher. I would love to
> know each one of you, so this survey is designed for me to know a little bit more about you
> and what your expectations are for Chinese class.

## 用中文回答
1. 你叫什么名字？
2. 你的生日是几月几号？你哪年出生？你几岁？
3. 你是哪国人？你住在哪儿？
4. 你会说什么语言 (languages)？

## 用英文回答
- What are two things that you want Ms. Zhang to know about you?
- What classroom activities or games that you have seen that you like?
- What do you expect from a good teacher?
- What are the skills that you want to learn and practice in this trimester?
  (Chinese character recognition / Pinyin and tones / Chinese character writing /
  Speaking skills / Chinese sentence writing / Listening skills / Chinese paragraph writing /
  Grammar / Radicals / Chinese culture)
- Please list at least two things that you are interested to learn about Chinese culture.
- If Chinese class had a chance to go on a field trip, where would you want to go?
- What expectations do you have for Chinese class in this school year?
- Is there anything you want to let Ms. Zhang know?
