# Cycle 6: 一个可怕的故事 (11/10 – 11/17)

## Overview
A ghost story ("the woman's story") for the Halloween season — watching a short video, retelling, drawing, and a quiz.

## 逐日细节（内部记录，不渲染到页面，见上面的 Slides 嵌入）

### 11/10
**Do-Now:** When is Halloween? What's your favorite candy?

**Objective:** I can talk about the scary story.

**Main Activities:**
- Reassessment quiz: My Day Is So Tiring
- Watch a short video: [A Scary Story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mU3vsjvP10w)
- Talk about the scary story

### 11/13
**Do-Now:** Where does the woman want to go?

**Objective:** I can talk about the woman's story.

**Main Activities:**
- Review new vocabulary; talk about the woman's story
- "Pencil Grab" game; draw & retell the woman's story

### 11/17
**Do-Now:** Why is the woman scared?

**Objective:** I can talk about the woman's story.

**Main Activities:**
- Review/read the woman's story; draw; talk about the woman's story
- Quiz
