# Cycle 5: Daily Routine Deep Dive → A Scary Story (10/21 – 11/20)

## 教学内容时间线

### 10/21
**Do-Now:** Grammar review with 竟然 (unexpectedly): "I guessed this year's Spring Festival would be in February, but it's actually in January."

**Objective:** I can draw my day.

**Main Activities:**
- Quiz re-assessment
- Review: parts of the day, daily routine, telling time
- Draw "my day"

### 10/23
**Do-Now:** 1. What time do you eat lunch today? 2. What time did you go home yesterday?

### 10/25
**Do-Now:** What time do you usually eat breakfast on Saturday and Sunday?

**Objective:** I can write correct sentences.

**Main Activities:**
- Review: parts of the day, daily routine, telling time
- Draw "my day"; write "my day" sentences

### 11/7
**Do-Now:** What do these words mean? 可怕 (scary) / 故事 (story) / 开/关 (open/close)

**Objective:** I can write correct sentences.

**Main Activities:**
- Review: parts of the day, daily routine, telling time
- True/false: "my day" sentences
- Read "A Scary Story"

### 11/12
**Do-Now:** Did you review "A Scary Story"? Can you tell the story?

**Objective:** I can retell "A Scary Story."

**Main Activities:**
- Read "A Scary Story"; speaking quiz — retell the story in your own words
- Draw "my day"

### 11/14
**Do-Now:** How is your day different from a Chinese middle schooler's day?

**Objective:** I can listen to a Chinese student's story.

**Main Activities:**
- Speaking: "my day"
- Listening: Liu Lulin's story

### 11/18
**Do-Now:** It's getting colder and colder — do you agree? (grammar: 越来越 + adjective)

### 11/20
**Do-Now:** Thanksgiving is getting closer — what do you want to do?

**Objective:** I can listen to a Chinese student's story.

**Main Activities:**
- Listening: Liu Lulin's story
