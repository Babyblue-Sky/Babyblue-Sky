# Cycle 1: 开学 / Unit 启动 (9/10 – 9/18)

## 教学内容时间线

### 9/10
**Do-Now:** Are you happy today, and why? (emotion vocabulary: nervous vs. excited)

**Objective:** I can introduce myself and understand our classroom norms.

**Main Activities:**
- Teacher self-introduction; classroom norms and language-learning philosophy
- "Find someone who" / this-or-that game comparing similarities among classmates
- Exit ticket: one thing you learned today

### 9/12
**Objective:** I can introduce someone (name, nationality, one ability, one personality trait), and tell the difference between 是 (shì) and 很 (hěn).

**Main Activities:**
- Syllabus introduction, classroom language, Super Eight high-frequency phrases
- Practice: 这个 (this one) vs. 那个 (that one)

### 9/16
**Do-Now:** Review of Super Eight classroom phrases

**Objective:** I can introduce a classmate using the same sentence pattern as before.

**Main Activities:**
- Diagnostic Test (see [the Diagnostic Test](../06-assessment/diagnostic-test.md))
- Game: 这个 vs. 那个

### 9/18
**Objective:** I can write my Chinese name and ask for classroom permission (e.g. to go to the bathroom, see the nurse, get water).

**Main Activities:**
- Writing practice: your Chinese name
- Strokes / calligraphy intro, with a YouTube teaching video (see [Strokes / Calligraphy](../04-culture/shufa-calligraphy.md))
- Telling-time greeting expressions (I'm very good / I'm okay / I'm great / How are you?)
- Sharing/commenting: saying you like something of a classmate's
