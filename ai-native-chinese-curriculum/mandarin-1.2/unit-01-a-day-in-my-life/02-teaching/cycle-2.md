# Cycle 2: 熊猫的故事 → 中秋节的故事 (9/22 – 10/4)

## 教学内容时间线

### 9/22
**Do-Now:** What time is it now? (review of telling time)

**Objective:** I can use new vocabulary: 报告 (bào gào, "report"), 钟 (zhōng, "clock").

### 9/26
**Objective:** I can describe someone's morning routine with a time and an activity.

**Main Activities:**
- Homework review, reading practice: 起床 (get up) / 吃饭 (eat) / 睡觉 (sleep)

### 9/30
**Objective:** I can talk about [the Panda's Story](../03-content/xiongmao-de-gushi.md).

**Main Activities:**
- Reading-comprehension warm-up: the panda wants to eat bamboo, but it's in Chinatown, New York
- Sketch key details of the story; write key words in Pinyin
- Describe someone's evening routine with a time and an activity

### 10/3
**Main Activities:**
- Summative Test (see [the Summative Test](../06-assessment/summative-test.md))

### 10/4
**Objective:** I can talk about [the Mid-Autumn Festival Story](../04-culture/zhongqiujie-de-gushi.md).

**Main Activities:**
- Cultural warm-up: Do you know what day the Mid-Autumn Festival is? Who's in the story?
- Listening (role play), speaking (movie talk), listening ("Pencil Grab" true/false game)
- Homework: review new vocabulary (Quizlet)
