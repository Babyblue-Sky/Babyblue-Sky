# Cycle 3: 中秋节的故事 (10/10 – 10/16)

## 教学内容时间线

### 10/10
**Do-Now:** Speaking warm-up on [the Panda's Story](../03-content/xiongmao-de-gushi.md): 1. baby panda 2. he is hungry 3. he stole Ms. Liao's money

**Objective:** I can talk about [the Mid-Autumn Festival Story](../04-culture/zhongqiujie-de-gushi.md).

**Main Activities:**
- Retell the Panda's Story; review/correct the Panda's Story quiz
- Read the Mid-Autumn Festival Story

### 10/14
**Do-Now:** Speaking warm-up: 1. ten suns 2. moon 3. hero

**Objective:** I can read the Mid-Autumn Festival Story.

**Main Activities:**
- Read new vocabulary; read the Mid-Autumn Festival Story
- "Pencil Grab" listening game; draw the story

### 10/16
**Do-Now:** Character reading review: 太阳 (sun) / 月亮 (moon) / 杀 (kill) / 坏人 (bad guy)

**Objective:** I can talk about the Mid-Autumn Festival Story with a classmate.

**Main Activities:**
- Review new vocabulary; read the story; true/false "Pencil Grab" game
- Speaking: picture talk; expressive reading practice (reading aloud with different emotions)
- Short quiz (reading comprehension); writing practice
