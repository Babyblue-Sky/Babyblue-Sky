## 句子练习 (Sentence Writing Practice)
用以上词汇造句，只能用中文写。注意语序：**主语 + 时间 + 动词** 或 **时间 + 主语 + 动词**。

1. I go to school at 8:10 in the morning.
2. I go to bed at 10 at night.
3. I have breakfast at 7:30 in the morning. I like to have breakfast at home.
4. In the morning, I shower and brush my teeth.
5. I like to eat healthy things.
6. I do homework every day at 4 in the afternoon.
7. I eat lunch at school every day at 12 noon.
8. My dad goes to work at 9 in the morning every day.
9. My mom goes home every day at 8:20 at night.
10. Yesterday my dad ate unhealthy things for dinner.
11. I like to eat salad because it is healthy.
