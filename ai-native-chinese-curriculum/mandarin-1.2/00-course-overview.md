# Mandarin 1.2 — Course Overview

> 本文件是 Course 层级的共享内容，Unit 1/2/3 都引用这里的 Standards 定义和年度大纲，
> 不在每个 Unit 里重复维护一份。

## Course Standards（Revised NYS Learning Standards for World Languages）
五条 Anchor Standard，贯穿全年、全部 Unit：

| ID | 名称 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| MS.WL.1 | Interpretive Communication | Learners understand, interpret, and analyze what is heard, read, received or viewed on a variety of topics, using a range of diverse texts, including authentic resources. |
| MS.WL.2 | Interpersonal Communication | Learners interact and negotiate meaning in spontaneous, spoken, visual, or written communication to exchange information and express feelings, preferences, and opinions. |
| MS.WL.3 | Presentational Communication | Learners present information and ideas on a variety of topics adapted to various audiences of listeners, readers, or viewers to describe, inform, narrate, explain, or persuade. |
| MS.WL.4 | Relating Cultural Practices and Products to Perspectives | Learners use the target language to identify, describe, and explain the practices and products of the culture studied as well as the cultural perspectives they suggest. |
| MS.WL.5 | Cultural Comparisons | Learners use the target language to compare the practices and products of the culture studied and their own. |

标准来源分组：Anchor Standard 1 = Communication（WL.1-3，input/output/交互）；
Anchor Standard 2 = Culture（WL.4-5）。

## Standards-Based Grading (SBLG)
评分等级：**EE**（Exceeding）/ **ME**（Meeting）/ **AE**（Approaching）/ **BE**（Below）。
每次主要评量（quiz/test/project）对应测量几条具体标准，全年多次测量同一标准，
给学生多次展示掌握程度的机会。

- **Formative Assessments**：记录在 Schoology，不计入期末成绩，用于给师生反馈。
- **Summative Assessments**（test / quiz / project / presentation）：计入期末成绩，通常安排在每个 trimester 结束前。

## Units of Study（年度大纲）

### Unit 1: A Day in My Life 我的一天
- Language forms: routine activities, time and date expressions, school subjects
- Language functions: describing one's daily routine in school and at home
- Final Project: infographic 或视频，主题「我的一天」
- 详见 [unit-01-a-day-in-my-life/01-overview.md](./unit-01-a-day-in-my-life/01-overview.md)

### Unit 2: Food and Culture 一日三餐
- Language forms: fruits and vegetables, meals, tastes of food, food preferences
- Language functions: describing one's food preferences and daily meals
- Assessments（Syllabus 记录，未验证是否为最新版本）：Zodiac Story Retell/Reading，
  Thunder's Story Retell/Reading，Describe a Photo/Picture (Interpersonal)，Story Writing
- Final Project: 一周美食日记 (week-long food journal)

### Unit 3: Story Telling 讲故事
- Language forms: past tense, sequence and connecting words, hobbies
- Language functions: retelling a story in the past tense
- Assessments（Syllabus 记录，未验证是否为最新版本）：The Monkey's Story Retell/Reading，

- Learning Objective: 通过成语和古代智慧故事，建立频率副词的概念，创作自己的故事
